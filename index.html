Who we are

Representing the people and businesses behind Europe's games.

ISFE — the Interactive Software Federation of Europe — represents the video games industry in Europe. We bring together national trade associations and leading companies to help ensure the value of games is understood by policymakers, partners and the public.

Our work supports a strong, innovative and responsible ecosystem, helping the sector contribute to Europe's creative and digital future.
